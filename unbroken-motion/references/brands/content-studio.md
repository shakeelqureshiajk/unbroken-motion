# Brand: Content Studio (`content-studio`)

Derived from the `warm-editorial` palette for the Content Creation explainer reel.

Tactile daylight paper aesthetic built for design studios, consumer storytelling reels, and editorial product launches.

## Visual Tone
- **Canvas:** Warm linen ivory (`#FBF7F0`) paired with soft sand cards (`#F3ECE0`) and pure paper elevation (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Hierarchy:** Classic high-contrast espresso ink (`#2F2620`) paired with terracotta primary (`#D96B43`) and forest emerald secondary (`#1F5C4D`).
- **Motion Feel:** Unhurried `soft` easing and slightly wider handheld breathing (`0.45`) with generous pill radii (`20px–30px`).
- **Forbidden:** Dark obsidian backgrounds and electric neon cyan/indigo belonging to `obsidian-tech`.
